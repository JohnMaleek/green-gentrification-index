Methodology & sources

GreenSense reports **measured** values from the Green Sentinel monitoring window (May 21 – June 19, 2026; DKV transit database, May 2026 statistics). Indices on this page are computed from those measurements. It contains no AQI / NDVI / satellite estimates and no 2023–2025 baseline — legacy-land-use labels are curated from the 1999/2020 Cívis GIStory base maps for visual comparison only.
