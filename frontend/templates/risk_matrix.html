Core Analytic Pillars
1
Development Pressure Identification

Areas like Petőfi tér receiving environmental benefits while maintaining heavy transit density (DKV junction) naturally become prime corridors for commercial redevelopment.

2
Targeted Urban Intervention

Enables municipal leaders to deploy green public housing and rent-stability covenants proactively, well before speculative market shifts materialize.

3
Public Health Equity

Ensures that legacy working populations residing in transitioning high-transit zones are protected and directly experience long-term air quality improvements.
